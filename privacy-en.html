SignalX Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026. Publisher: kh_zw. Release: 1.4.0 (12). Privacy contact: khzw.apps@gmail.com. Developer website: https://khkh2269-35.github.io/.

SignalX Pro helps you inspect Android cellular readings and save optional coverage sessions. It contains Google AdMob banners on the Home, Cells and Coverage screens only, and Google UMP for privacy choices where required. No account or developer-operated server is needed. Google's advertising and consent data handling is explained below.

Cellular observations and local records

With your permission, the app reads cellular network information made available by Android: selected SIM/subscription and slot, operator information, serving/neighbor cell details, technology, band/frequency information when available, signal measurements, and timestamps. Android can require phone-state and precise-location permission and enabled device location for CellInfo even when GPS positions are not being recorded. Granting that permission does not itself start GPS recording.

Live histories are maintained on device. Explicitly saved coverage sessions may contain timestamps, subscription/slot references, cell references, radio metrics, notes and optional sampled device latitude/longitude and accuracy. Saved cells, place comparisons, outage observations, alert preferences and complaint drafts are also local features. The app does not send these cellular records, GPS positions or notes to its developer or Google advertising requests.

Optional network monitoring is a separate action. It uses a visible foreground notification to continue CellInfo observations for the chosen duration; it does not enable GPS sampling or create a saved coverage session. Coverage recording starts only after your explicit session choice. Both position-enabled and radio-only sessions use a visible foreground-service notification so recording may continue after you leave the app or lock the screen. In radio-only mode, only signal readings are saved; no GPS coordinates are sampled or stored. Saving positions requires a separate, explicit opt-in. You can stop these tasks in the app or through the available notification controls. Android or manufacturer battery controls can interrupt them. No “allow all the time” background-location grant is requested.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google Mobile Ads collects and shares data such as the device's IP address (which can indicate general location), app/ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and applicable identifiers such as app set ID. This app configuration removes advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox permissions, requests non-personalized ads and disables publisher first-party ID. These controls do not prevent all SDK data processing. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Personalized, non-personalized or limited advertising behavior depends on your choices, local requirements and the publisher's actual configuration; non-personalized ads do not mean no data is processed. There is no separate developer analytics service.

UMP manages applicable privacy messages and choices. Use the app's privacy-options control when available to revisit choices. Android settings may allow you to reset or delete the advertising ID. Cellular/location permission is used for cellular features, not ad targeting; the app does not supply precise GPS or cell identities to ads. Google's data practices and controls: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. This candidate integrates Google AdMob and UMP directly and includes no mediation adapters or separately added mediation partners. Applicable messages and privacy options depend on your region and the publisher's AdMob configuration. Any addition of advertising partners requires a review of this disclosure before release.

Google Play update check

When the app is open and no recording, monitoring or manual network check is active, it may ask Google Play whether a newer version is available. Google documents processing of device metadata, the app version and installed module/asset-pack information for this check, using encryption and a fixed retention period. No specific retention duration is stated here. SignalX does not attach cell readings, SIM identities, GPS positions or saved sessions to this request. The notice is optional; only choosing Update opens the store. There is no background update polling or closed-app push notification.

Optional HTTPS connection check

You can manually run an HTTPS response check to a hostname you confirm. That host and network/DNS services may receive connection metadata including IP address and request timing; the host's own policy governs retention. This does not measure download or upload throughput.

Exporting, retention and deletion

CSV/PDF export and Android sharing occur when you request them. Review included fields and any coordinate option before sharing. Other apps, selected recipients and storage/cloud services handle exported copies under their own policies. The app's local deletion controls do not remove external copies.

Sessions remain in private app storage until you delete them or the app prunes them according to its retention settings and storage limits. Retention is not a guaranteed background deletion deadline. Session deletion and “Delete all sessions & saved cells” apply to the data named in those controls; other local features may have their own deletion controls. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes app-private records and preferences. It does not erase exported files, recipient copies, or provider-held data. System app-data backup is disabled. Google's retention and deletion practices are governed by its own policies; no fixed duration is promised here.

Security, children and contact

Advertising, consent and the optional HTTPS check use HTTPS/TLS transport. Local private storage and Android permission controls limit access, but no security method is absolute. No measurement account is created, so there is no in-app account to delete.

SignalX Pro is intended for adults aged 18 and over, as confirmed by the developer. It is not directed to children.

For questions about app-private data or this policy, contact khzw.apps@gmail.com. If the policy changes, the developer will update this page and its effective date.
